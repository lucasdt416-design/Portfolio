Featured project · Independent · 2026 – present

StockPulse

why I built it

Following the market, I kept hitting the same wall. The news that actually moves a stock is scattered: a filing lands on the SEC site, the company posts a release, and five outlets rewrite the same story. Nothing tells you which one matters. I wanted one place that pulls it all in, drops the repeats, and tells you plainly what's worth your attention. So I built it.

StockPulse · Sorting & scoring

Duplicates out.
Signal in.

The same story shows up in five places at once. A deduplication engine collapses the repeats, then a rule-based classifier scores what's left from 0 to 10, with the arithmetic shown. The brightest shelves are the high-priority ones.
